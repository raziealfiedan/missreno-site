Questions

The questions we hear most.

What does MissReno do?
MissReno is a one-stop renovation centre in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. We supply wall and ceiling finishes such as fluted panels, marble-look and stone-look sheets and soffit ceilings, and our own team installs them.
Where is MissReno located?
MissReno is at Lot 2, Ground Floor, Lorong Durian 3, Jalan Tuaran, Kian Yap Industrial Estate, 88450 Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. See the showroom page
What are the showroom opening hours?
The showroom is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed on Sunday.
Do I need a full renovation?
No. Many projects upgrade a single wall or ceiling. Send us a photo and we will suggest what suits the space.
How much does a feature wall cost?
It depends on the finish and the size. The Christmas promotion, 1 to 30 Nov 2026, starts at RM1,899 with installation and SST included for a wall of about 3 m × 3 m. Send a photo for an exact quote.
How long does installation take?
It depends on the size and the finish. Send us a photo and we will confirm the schedule with your quote.
Is it messy?
Our team keeps the work tidy and clears the area when the job is done. Tell us about your space and we will explain what to expect.
Do you deliver outside Kota Kinabalu?
Yes, across Sabah, including Tuaran, Penampang, Putatan, Papar, Kudat, Sandakan and Tawau. Quotes outside Kota Kinabalu depend on distance.
Do you offer trade pricing?
Yes, for contractors, designers and businesses. Show your business registration once and we will set up a trade account. See trade accounts
